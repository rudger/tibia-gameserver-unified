💡 Combat Guide, Damage Formulas & Calculations (CipSoft 7.72)

⚔️ 1. Melee Attack & Physical Damage

Every combat round (~2 seconds), the monster performs a physical attack against its target:

Raw_Damage = random(0, Attack)

Next, the target's Defense (Defend) value tries to block the blow:

Damage_After_Shield = max(0, Raw_Damage - Target_Defense)

If Damage_After_Shield > 0, the target's Armor absorbs part of the impact:

Armor_Absorption = (Armor / 2) + random(0, Armor / 2)
Final_Damage = max(0, Damage_After_Shield - Armor_Absorption)

🛡️ 2. Defense & Resistances

  • Defend: Acts as shield defense. Blocks physical damage from up to 2 attackers per round.
  • Armor: Absorbs residual damage. On average absorbs 75% of Armor value on each physical hit.
  • Poison: If Poison > 0, when landing a physical hit the monster inflicts poison damage from Poison/2 to Poison per second.
  • Blood: Type of blood emitted (Blood = Red, Slime = Green, Undead = Smoke, Fire = Flames, Energy = Blue).

✨ 3. Spells & Ranged Magic Attacks

Spells formatted as Shape -> Impact : Delay are evaluated each turn:

  • Shape: How the spell is cast:
    • Origin(Missile, Effect): Straight line projectile.
    • Victim(Missile, Range, Effect): Locked-on target projectile.
    • Actor(Effect): Centered on monster (Self Buff / Heal).
    • Destination(...): Ground area / Bomb.
    • Angle(...): Frontal cone / wave (e.g. Dragon Wave).
  • Impact: The resulting effect (Damage(Type, Max, Min), Healing, Speed/Paralyze, Summon, Field).
  • Delay (Turns): Spell frequency. : 2 = 50% chance/turn (~every 4s), : 6 = ~16% chance/turn (~every 12s).

💬 4. Monster Talk Lines & Yells

  • Regular Talk (Say): Appears in yellow over the creature within normal screen vision.
  • Yell (#Y): Starts with #Y . Appears in red to all players across the full game screen (e.g. #Y GROOAAARRR!).
